Questions

Where is my data stored?+

In your browser's local storage. Nothing is sent to a server, so clearing your browser data removes your saved cards and logo.

Do the QR codes expire?+

No. A code holds your link or text directly, so it works as long as that link works. PIN-locked codes need this site to stay online to open the unlock page.

How secure is the PIN?+

Content is encrypted with your PIN inside the code. A longer PIN is safer, because someone who has the code can try guesses offline.
